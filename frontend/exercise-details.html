Chest

Barbell Bench Press

Target muscles:
Chest, front shoulders, triceps
Equipment:
Barbell, flat bench
Difficulty:
Intermediate

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Lie back on a flat bench with your eyes under the bar.
  2. Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width.
  3. Unrack the bar and lower it slowly to your mid-chest.
  4. Press the bar back up to the starting position.

⚠️ Safety Tips & Common Mistakes

  • Keep your feet firmly planted on the floor.
  • Do not bounce the bar off your chest.
  • Use a spotter when lifting heavy.
Back

Pull-Ups

Target muscles:
Lats, biceps, upper back
Equipment:
Pull-up bar
Difficulty:
Intermediate

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Hang from the bar with an overhand grip, slightly wider than your shoulders.
  2. Pull your shoulder blades down and back.
  3. Pull yourself up until your chin clears the bar.
  4. Lower yourself under control to a full hang.

⚠️ Safety Tips & Common Mistakes

  • Avoid swinging or kicking your legs.
  • Do not shrug your shoulders up to your ears.
  • Use a resistance band if full reps are too hard.
Legs

Barbell Squats

Target muscles:
Quads, glutes, hamstrings
Equipment:
Barbell, squat rack
Difficulty:
Intermediate

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Set the bar on your upper back, feet shoulder-width apart.
  2. Brace your core and push your hips back and down.
  3. Descend until your thighs are parallel to the floor.
  4. Drive through your heels to stand back up.

⚠️ Safety Tips & Common Mistakes

  • Keep your knees tracking over your toes.
  • Keep your chest up and back neutral.
  • Set the safety bars at the right height.

Shoulders

Overhead Press

Target muscles:
Shoulders, triceps, upper chest
Equipment:
Barbell or dumbbells
Difficulty:
Intermediate

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Hold the weight at shoulder height with your elbows slightly forward.
  2. Squeeze your glutes and brace your core.
  3. Press the weight straight overhead until your arms lock out.
  4. Lower it back to your shoulders with control.

⚠️ Safety Tips & Common Mistakes

  • Do not lean back excessively.
  • Keep your wrists straight and stacked over your elbows.
  • Start light to learn the movement.

Back

Deadlift

Target muscles:
Lower back, glutes, hamstrings
Equipment:
Barbell
Difficulty:
Advanced

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Stand with the bar over mid-foot, feet hip-width apart.
  2. Hinge at the hips and grip the bar just outside your legs.
  3. Brace your core and push the floor away to stand up.
  4. Lower the bar by hinging at the hips, keeping it close to your legs.

⚠️ Safety Tips & Common Mistakes

  • Never round your lower back.
  • Keep the bar close to your body.
  • Learn the form with light weight first.

Shoulders

Dumbbell Lateral Raise

Target muscles:
Side shoulders
Equipment:
Dumbbells
Difficulty:
Beginner

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Stand holding a dumbbell in each hand at your sides.
  2. With a slight bend in your elbows, raise your arms out to the sides.
  3. Stop when your arms reach shoulder height.
  4. Lower slowly back to your sides.

⚠️ Safety Tips & Common Mistakes

  • Use a light weight and avoid swinging.
  • Do not raise above shoulder height.
  • Keep your shoulders relaxed, away from your ears.
